# Design Brief — The Drawdown Protocol

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)
You are an AI simulation sent by future humanity to solve the 21st-century climate crisis. Each run replays 2030 to 2100 on an isometric bioregion: one policy per year, every choice a trade-off between emissions, carbon sinks, and social resilience. Push past +2.0 C of warming or let resilience collapse and the run ends — but every failure yields Insight, unlocking new paradigms (Circular Economy, Universal Basic Services, Degrowth) that make the next timeline winnable. It is a rogue-lite where the enemy is physics and the reward is a beautiful, sober world.

## Player Fantasy
"I am the strategist of a livable future." The player embodies a benevolent optimizer who sees a whole region across a whole century, makes hard calls with imperfect information, and watches the world visibly transform — from grey sprawl and smog to green tram lines, rooftop gardens, and rewilded tiles. Failure is not punishment; it is data. Mastery feels like understanding a system, not exploiting a glitch.

## Player Goals
- **In a run:** reach 2100 with resilience above zero and warming contribution below +2.0 C.
- **Across runs:** earn Insight Points, unlock paradigms in the Paradigm Hub, and discover new viable strategies.
- **Emotionally:** move from "everything is on fire" to "I can see the path" — hope earned through competence.

## Win / Fail States
| State | Condition | Player reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Win** | Year reaches 2100 | "The region made it through the century." |
| **Loss A** | Resilience <= 0 | "Society broke before the climate did." |
| **Loss B** | Warming contribution >= +2.0 C | "Tipping points breached; the simulation diverges." |
| **Always** | Run end grants Insight Points | "Even a failed timeline teaches the Protocol." |

## Target Audience
- **Primary:** players of strategic management and rogue-lite games (Frostpunk, Terra Nil, Slay the Spire, Balatro) who enjoy readable systems and build variety. PC, mouse-first, sessions of 10–20 minutes.
- **Secondary:** climate-curious players and educators drawn to a science-grounded, non-doomer take (IPCC AR6 sufficiency, Project Drawdown solutions, EAT-Lancet diet shift).
- **Not targeting:** hardcore grand-strategy players expecting deep economic simulation, or players seeking pure sandbox/city-builder creativity.

## Session Shape
- One full run: 10–20 minutes, 71 yearly turns (2030–2100 inclusive), most turns resolved in seconds.
- First 2 turns must teach the core loop by playing it (pick a card, watch the metrics move, read the log).
- Run end always flows into the Paradigm Hub: spend Insight, start a new timeline.

## Tone and Look
Solarpunk, not apocalypse porn. The early board is harsh and grey; the optimized board is lush, advanced, and harmonious. The game argues that sufficiency is aspirational, and it argues through visuals and systems, never through lecture text.

## One-Line Player Promise
*Every year, one hard choice; every run, a clearer picture of how to save the century.*
