# Design Brief — The Drawdown Protocol

## Elevator Pitch (30 seconds)

You lead a small group with a huge mission: an NGO — or a small pioneering country — that must steer the whole world to carbon neutrality between 2030 and 2100. Each year you make one strategic move: transform industry, transport, or the agro-economy, restore carbon sinks, fund media and adaptation, or spend Influence to win allies whose money accelerates everything. Heat waves, mega fires, tsunamis, and social crises stress-test what you build — and every disaster is also a chance to rebuild better. Cross +2.0 °C and the run is lost; but the Knowledge you gained persists, and the next timeline starts smarter.

## Player Fantasy

"We are the ones who showed the world it was possible." The player is a **group of people**, not an abstract force: a credible small actor with limited money and outsized conviction. They cannot command other countries — they convince them. The fantasy has three beats:

- **Persuasion over power:** alliances and joint projects, earned with Influence.
- **Stewardship of a budget:** every unit of money spent where it moves the needle most.
- **Turning crisis into opportunity:** a burned forest becomes a resilient one; a flooded highway becomes a rail line; a social crisis opens the window for a fairer policy.

## Player Goals

- **In a run:** reach 2100 with the world carbon-neutral (emissions ≤ absorption) while keeping warming below +2.0 °C, money solvent, and people happy enough to sustain the model.
- **Across runs:** earn Knowledge Points and unlock the Knowledge tree (affordable EVs, healthy sobriety, informed public...) so future runs start with real insight.
- **Emotionally:** move from "the whole world is against us" to "the whole world is with us" — hope earned through cooperation and competence.

## Win / Fail States

| State | Condition | Player reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Win** | Year 2100 reached with net emissions ≤ 0 and warming < +2.0 °C | "The world is carbon-neutral. The balance holds." |
| **Loss A (hard)** | Warming reaches **+2.0 °C** at any point | "The limit for a safe human world is breached." (Paris Agreement ceiling; see Concept.md sources) |
| **Loss B (soft)** | Year 2100 reached but net emissions still > 0 | "We survived the century, but the job is not done." |
| **Warning** | Warming crosses **+1.5 °C** → **Overshoot** state | Events intensify; sinks degrade; the pressure is on. Not a loss — a klaxon. |
| **Always** | Run end grants Knowledge Points (more for wins) | "Even a failed timeline teaches us something permanent." |

There is deliberately **one hard loss condition** (warming), so defeat is always readable. Social collapse hurts through the systems — low happiness cuts income, triggers social crises, and costs allies — rather than through a second instant game over.

## Target Audience

- **Primary:** players of strategic management and rogue-lite games (Frostpunk, Terra Nil, Slay the Spire, Balatro) who enjoy readable systems, build variety, and meaningful trade-offs. PC, mouse-first, runs of 10–20 minutes.
- **Secondary:** climate-curious players and educators drawn to a science-grounded, cooperative, non-doomer take (Paris Agreement thresholds, IPCC sufficiency, Project Drawdown solutions, EAT-Lancet diet shift).
- **Not targeting:** hardcore grand-strategy players expecting deep per-country economic simulation, or players seeking pure sandbox creativity.

## Session Shape

- One run: 71 yearly turns (2030–2100 inclusive), 10–20 minutes, most turns resolved in seconds.
- The first two turns must teach the loop by playing it: pick a card, watch the three pillars move, read the log.
- Run end always flows into the Knowledge tree: spend Knowledge Points, start a new timeline on a freshly generated world map.

## One-Line Player Promise

*Every year, one wise move; every ally, a faster transition; every defeat, knowledge the world keeps.*
