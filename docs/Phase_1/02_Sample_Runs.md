# Sample Runs — The Drawdown Protocol (Phase 1)

Three scripted strategies simulated over the full 2030–2100 timeline with the formulas in
`01_Balance_Model.md`, seed **2030** (deterministic; a throwaway script generated these
tables and stayed out of the repo per golden rule 4). All three assume a first run:
**no Knowledge nodes active**.

Together they satisfy the Phase 1 pacing check: at least one viable winning strategy,
multiple losing paths, and pressure that peaks mid-to-late century.

Column key: Emissions/Absorption/Net in GtCO2e/yr; Ind/Tra/Agr = sector transition %.
Table rows are end-of-decade snapshots.

## Cross-Run Pacing Read

- **Early game (2030s):** learning space — low event pressure, first cards teach the loop.
- **Mid game (2040s–2060s):** pressure — Overshoot begins, fires scar sinks, happiness dips.
- **Late game (2070s–2090s):** climax or bloom — winners watch the world visibly recover;
  the tech-rush path faces cascading feedback loops and dies at the doorstep of 2100.
- The +1.5 °C warning is crossed by *every* strategy (≈ 2038–2040): Overshoot is the
  designed mid-game state, not a rare failure — what matters is how deep and how long.
